Production standard / 001

Responsible AI
starts with people.

Balamonis uses AI as part of a directed production process. Human creators remain responsible for the story, selections, approvals, rights, and final release.

01

Human direction

Scripts, visual direction, shot selection, editing, sound, color, and final approval are treated as creative decisions—not hidden automation. Production history is preserved wherever the workflow supports it.

02

Consent and likeness

Real-person faces, voices, performances, and digital replicas require specific permission for the intended use. Balamonis does not support deceptive impersonation or non-consensual cloning.

03

Rights and sources

Productions should record the source, license, provider, model, date, permitted use, territory, and consent status for important assets. Unresolved rights questions must be cleared before release.

04

Truthful delivery

Output specifications are described accurately. “4K mastered” is not presented as “native 4K generation,” development studies are not presented as commissioned work, and future capabilities are labeled as planned.

05

Disclosure and provenance

AI assistance should be disclosed when context, platform policy, a client agreement, or audience trust calls for it. Balamonis is working toward exportable provenance and rights reports for every production.

06

Human release gate

No agent should independently publish a film, spend money, sign a contract, grant rights, imitate a real person, or send a buyer pitch. A responsible human owns every external action.

This page describes the studio’s operating direction and is not legal advice. Production-specific requirements are confirmed in the applicable agreement.
