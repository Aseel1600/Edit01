# Balamonis Founding Original — Sprint 01

- Sprint window: August 13–26, 2026 (10 business days)
- Owner: Founder / Executive Producer
- Pipeline: Cinematic
North-star outcome: one approved, culturally grounded, rights-ready founding Original package that is ready for controlled visual proof production.

## Sprint goal

Approve the creative and commercial foundation for a 7–9 minute Balamonis Original, then convert the approved production workflow into the first Balamonis Creator workspace contract.

This sprint ends with an approved script and production-ready scene plan—not an unreviewed pile of generated clips. A visual proof may begin only after its preceding human gates and provider setup are complete.

## Capacity and scope

The sprint is intentionally loaded to roughly 75% of available capacity. The remaining margin covers provider setup, research surprises, rights questions, and creative revision.

### P0 — must ship

- Founder approves one concept, cultural/language anchor, Remotion runtime, atelier composition mode, and proof-spend ceiling.
- Exact tool path for the proof is either configured or consciously deferred.
- A 7–9 minute screenplay passes story review and founder approval.
- Story, character, location, prop, wardrobe, voice, and visual-continuity rules are recorded.
- Scene plan names every purposeful scene, shot intention, source/generation path, and rights requirement.
- Balamonis Creator workspace schema is validated against the founding project.
- Human-authorship journal and rights ledger begin before assets are created.

### P1 — should ship

- Eight-clip visual-proof prompts, reference frames, seed policy, and quality rubric are ready.
- One music direction is selected with license evidence and cue-sheet placeholder.
- One consented human-performance plan is ready.
- Trailer, vertical cutdown, case-study, and social-derivative requirements are mapped from the master story.
- A buyer-facing delivery checklist is attached to the project.

### Stretch

- Generate and review the capped 20–30 second proof after all earlier gates pass.
- Publish a private product walkthrough using the project workspace data.
- Secure two creator interviews and one brand/agency pilot conversation using the founding workflow.

## Daily execution

### Day 1 — Thursday, August 13

- Founder decision: concept, culture/language, runtime/mode, and proof budget.
- Record the decision in the production log.
- If the cloud proof is approved, add `FAL_KEY` to the local `.env`; never post the key in chat or commit it.
- Define three non-negotiable emotional outcomes for the film.

Exit condition: proposal gate is approved or returned with specific changes.

### Day 2 — Friday, August 14

- Write the one-page story treatment, protagonist need, opposition, turn, climax, and final image.
- Define the cultural-consultation and authenticity checks.
- Start title search; treat every current title as a working title until cleared.
- Map the master into trailer, three vertical episodes, and one making-of piece.

Exit condition: treatment is coherent enough to draft without unanswered premise questions.

### Day 3 — Monday, August 17

- Write screenplay draft 1.
- Read it aloud and time it.
- Remove exposition that can be carried by action, object, silence, or sound.
- Record every meaningful human writing and selection decision in the authorship journal.

Exit condition: complete beginning-to-end screenplay draft.

### Day 4 — Tuesday, August 18

- Story review against character desire, causal progression, emotional turn, and ending.
- Revise dialogue for a specific lived voice.
- Confirm no real person's face, voice, likeness, or biography will be synthesized without explicit permission.
- Present the script at its human approval gate.

Exit condition: approved script or a bounded revision list.

### Day 5 — Wednesday, August 19

- Build character, location, wardrobe, prop, lens, palette, lighting, and sound bibles.
- Give every recurring element a stable ID.
- Define reference-image requirements and prohibited imitation patterns.
- Add source, license, consent, territory, term, and permitted-use fields to every planned asset.

Exit condition: continuity bible covers every recurring visual and audible element.

### Day 6 — Thursday, August 20

- Convert the approved script into 45–70 purposeful shots.
- For each shot, record dramatic purpose, duration, framing, camera behavior, performance, sound, continuity dependencies, and production path.
- Classify expensive or high-risk shots and design simpler alternatives that preserve story meaning.

Exit condition: complete scene plan with no decorative filler shots.

### Day 7 — Friday, August 21

- Review the scene plan for eyelines, geography, time, wardrobe, props, light, weather, character state, and sound continuity.
- Estimate candidate generations and cost per approved second.
- Select the eight proof shots that best test face, motion, performance, lighting, dialogue/ambience, and continuity.
- Present the scene plan at its human approval gate.

Exit condition: approved scene plan or a bounded correction list.

### Day 8 — Monday, August 24

- Prepare twelve reference frames and eight proof prompts only after prior approvals.
- Lock provider, model, duration, aspect ratio, resolution, seed policy, sample cost, and retry ceiling.
- Select a music candidate and archive its license evidence; no track is treated as cleared by search alone.
- Obtain approval for the asset strategy and first sample spend.

Exit condition: asset gate is ready, with maximum proof spend of $12.52 unless the founder changes it.

### Day 9 — Tuesday, August 25

- If authorized and configured, generate the eight-clip proof—no batch generation.
- Review identity, anatomy, motion, physics, lip sync, camera, lighting, audio, temporal artifacts, and emotional truth.
- Record actual cost and accepted seconds.
- Stop if the sample misses the quality floor; revise the plan before spending more.

Exit condition: proof is approved, rejected, or returned with precise retake criteria.

### Day 10 — Wednesday, August 26

- Run sprint review and rights/authorship audit.
- Demonstrate the founding project through the Balamonis Creator workspace structure.
- Decide Sprint 02: production batch, contained live-action hybrid, or open-source GPU-worker pilot.
- Publish a private weekly report: shipped, learned, costs, audience/revenue opportunity, risks, and decisions required.

Exit condition: Sprint 02 has a clear goal and no hidden provider, rights, or budget assumption.

## Definition of done

- Research brief and proposal validate against repository schemas.
- Every creative gate has a recorded human decision.
- Script and scene plan are approved and versioned.
- Every recurring character, voice, location, prop, costume, and music cue has a rights/consent state.
- Every generated or captured asset can record source, provider/model, creation date, license, consent, and provenance.
- Tool spend is visible before each generation batch; actual spend is recorded afterward.
- No motion-required promise silently degrades to stills.
- Remotion remains the locked runtime unless a new visible decision is approved.
- The master plan includes 1080p delivery, captions, clean/textless elements, audio stems, cue sheet, QC, provenance, and buyer-facing rights status.
